# Benefits and Wellness

This policy describes the benefits Acme Corp offers its employees, including health insurance, retirement savings, the learning budget and wellness support, and explains how and when to enrol.

Policy owner: People team (Benefits).

## Eligibility

Permanent employees and fixed-term employees on contracts of 6 months or longer are eligible for the benefits in this policy from their first day of employment. Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week receive the same benefits as full-time employees, except for the learning budget and wellness stipend, which are pro rata.

## Enrolment

New employees must enrol in benefits within 30 days of their start date using the benefits section of PeopleHub. If you do not enrol within 30 days, you are automatically placed on the Basic health plan with employee-only cover and you cannot change plans until the next open enrolment.

Open enrolment runs once a year from 1 to 30 November. Changes made during open enrolment take effect on 1 January.

### Life Events

Outside open enrolment, you may change your benefits within 30 days of a qualifying life event, such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, or a partner losing their own cover.

## Health Insurance

Acme Corp offers three health plan options. All plans cover hospital care, specialist visits, prescription medicines and mental health treatment. They differ in deductibles, provider networks and the share of the premium Acme Corp pays.

### Basic Plan

A high-deductible plan with a national provider network. Acme Corp pays 100% of the employee's premium and 80% of the premium for dependants. The annual deductible is $2,000 for an individual.

### Plus Plan

A mid-level plan with a wider network and a lower deductible of $750 for an individual. Acme Corp pays 80% of the premium for the employee and for dependants.

### Premium Plan

A low-deductible plan with the widest network, including out-of-network cover, and a deductible of $250 for an individual. Acme Corp pays 70% of the premium for the employee and for dependants.

The employee's share of the premium is deducted from salary before tax where the law allows.

### Dental and Vision

Dental and vision cover are included in all three health plans. Routine dental check-ups are covered twice a year, and one eye test and a $150 allowance for glasses or contact lenses are covered every 12 months.

## Retirement Savings

Acme Corp contributes to a retirement savings plan for every eligible employee. Acme Corp matches employee contributions dollar for dollar up to 5% of base salary. Employees are automatically enrolled at a contribution of 3% of base salary and may change their contribution at any time in PeopleHub.

Company matching contributions vest immediately.

## Life and Disability Insurance

- Life insurance: Acme Corp provides life insurance cover of 3 times base salary at no cost to the employee.
- Long-term disability insurance: replaces 60% of base salary after 90 days of disability, at no cost to the employee.

## Learning and Development Budget

Each full-time employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year for courses, conferences, certifications and books related to their current or future role at Acme Corp.

To use the budget, request approval from your manager in PeopleHub before booking. Approved costs are claimed through the expense process in the Travel and Expense policy (POL-06), or booked directly by the People team.

Unused learning budget does not carry over into the next year. If you leave Acme Corp within 12 months of completing a course costing more than $1,000, you may be asked to repay a pro rata share of the cost.

Employees also receive 3 paid learning days per year to attend approved training.

## Wellness Stipend

Every employee receives a wellness stipend of $50 per month, paid through payroll. It can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment, meditation apps or similar wellness costs. No receipts are needed, but the stipend is taxable where the law requires.

## Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)

The Employee Assistance Programme provides free, confidential support 24 hours a day for employees and their household members. It includes up to 6 free counselling sessions per issue per year, legal and financial guidance, and support for caring responsibilities. Acme Corp does not receive any information about who uses the EAP.

Contact details for the EAP are on the intranet and on the back of your benefits card.

## Family Support

In addition to parental leave (see the Sick and Family Leave policy, POL-04), Acme Corp reimburses up to $5,000 of eligible adoption or surrogacy costs per child, and offers 10 days of emergency back-up childcare per year through an approved provider.

## Employee Discounts

Employees receive a 30% discount on Acme Corp products for personal use, and access to a discount platform with offers from partner brands. Discounts may not be used to buy products for resale.

## Additional PTO Purchase

During open enrolment, employees may buy up to 5 additional days of PTO, as described in the Paid Time Off (PTO) policy (POL-02).

## Benefits When Leaving Acme Corp

Health, dental and vision cover continue until the last day of the month in which employment ends. The wellness stipend and learning budget end on the last day of employment. Retirement savings remain the employee's property.

## Questions

For questions about benefits, raise a PeopleHub ticket with the Benefits team or attend the monthly benefits drop-in session.
