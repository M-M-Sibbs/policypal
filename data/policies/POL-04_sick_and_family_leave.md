# Sick and Family Leave

This policy covers paid sick leave, parental leave, bereavement leave, caregiver leave and other family-related absences at Acme Corp. Annual paid time off is covered in the Paid Time Off (PTO) policy (POL-02).

Policy owner: People team.

## Eligibility

Paid sick leave applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees from their first day. Parental leave at the enhanced company rates in this policy applies after 6 months of continuous service; employees with less service receive the statutory minimum for their location.

## Paid Sick Leave

Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year at full pay. Part-time employees receive a pro rata amount.

Sick leave does not accrue month by month; the full annual amount is available from 1 January (or from the start date for new joiners, pro rata to the remaining months of the year). Unused sick days are not carried over and are not paid out when employment ends.

Sick leave may be used for your own illness or injury, medical and dental appointments that cannot reasonably be scheduled outside working hours, and recovery from planned medical procedures.

### Extended Sickness

If you exhaust your 10 paid sick days, further absence due to illness is paid at 50% of normal pay for up to 20 additional working days in the calendar year, provided you supply a doctor's note. After that, any statutory sick pay applies.

## Reporting Sickness

If you are too ill to work, you must notify your manager directly by phone or chat before 10:00 on the first day of absence, or as soon as possible if you are too unwell to do so. An email or message to a team channel alone is not sufficient.

You must also record the absence in PeopleHub on your first day back at work.

### Doctor's Note Threshold

A doctor's note (medical certificate) is required for any sickness absence of more than 3 consecutive calendar days. The note must be uploaded to PeopleHub within 2 working days of your return.

For absences of 3 consecutive calendar days or fewer, a self-certification in PeopleHub is sufficient.

Acme Corp may also request a doctor's note if an employee has more than 4 separate periods of sickness absence in 12 months.

### Return to Work

After any sickness absence of more than 5 working days, your manager will hold a short return-to-work conversation to check whether you need any support or adjustments, such as a phased return.

## Parental Leave

Acme Corp supports all parents, including birth parents, adoptive parents, and parents through surrogacy.

### Primary Caregiver Leave

The primary caregiver of a new child is entitled to 16 weeks of parental leave at full pay. This may be followed by up to 10 further weeks of unpaid leave.

Primary caregiver leave must start no earlier than 4 weeks before the expected birth or placement date and must be taken in a single continuous block.

### Secondary Caregiver Leave

The secondary caregiver is entitled to 6 weeks of parental leave at full pay. Secondary caregiver leave may be taken in up to 2 blocks, and must be completed within 12 months of the birth or placement.

### Notifying Acme Corp

Tell your manager and the People team of your intention to take parental leave at least 15 weeks before the expected birth or placement date, or as soon as reasonably possible in the case of adoption. Submit the parental leave request in PeopleHub.

### Keeping-in-Touch Days

During parental leave you may work up to 10 optional keeping-in-touch days, paid at your normal daily rate, without ending your leave. These days are entirely voluntary.

### Returning from Parental Leave

You have the right to return to the same role, or to a role with the same pay and seniority if your role no longer exists. Parents returning from primary caregiver leave may work a reduced schedule of 80% of normal hours at full pay for their first 4 weeks back.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees receive up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member: a spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or a parent of a spouse or partner.

Employees receive up to 2 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of another relative or a close friend.

Employees who experience a pregnancy loss, or whose partner does, receive 10 days of paid leave.

Bereavement leave does not need to be taken consecutively but should be taken within 3 months of the death.

## Caregiver Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid caregiver leave per calendar year to care for a dependant who is ill or injured, or to deal with an unexpected disruption to care arrangements. A dependant is a spouse, partner, child, parent, or someone who relies on you for care.

Caregiver leave is recorded in PeopleHub under the leave type "Caregiver".

## Other Paid Leave

- **Jury duty:** paid at full pay for the duration of service. Provide a copy of the summons to the People team.
- **Medical appointments during pregnancy:** paid time off for all appointments recommended by a medical professional, for both the pregnant employee and their partner (partner limited to 2 appointments).
- **Volunteering:** 2 days per year of paid volunteering leave for registered charities, booked in PeopleHub at least 10 working days in advance.

## Unpaid Leave

Employees may request up to 20 working days of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons. Requests are approved at the manager's discretion, considering business needs. PTO accrual pauses during unpaid leave of more than 10 consecutive working days, as described in POL-02.

## Confidentiality of Health Information

Medical certificates and health information are classified as Restricted under the Data Privacy and Retention policy (POL-09). They are visible only to the People team and are never shared with colleagues without your consent.
