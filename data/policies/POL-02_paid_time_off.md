# Paid Time Off (PTO)

This policy explains how much paid time off (PTO) Acme Corp employees receive, how it accrues, how to request it, and what happens to unused days. Public holidays are covered separately in the Public Holidays policy (POL-03), and sickness and family leave are covered in the Sick and Family Leave policy (POL-04).

Policy owner: People team.

## Eligibility

All permanent and fixed-term employees are eligible for PTO from their first day of employment, including employees who are still on probation. Contractors and agency workers are not covered by this policy.

## Annual Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per calendar year. After five years of continuous service, the entitlement increases to 25 days per calendar year, starting from the January after the fifth service anniversary.

Part-time employees receive PTO pro rata to their contracted hours. For example, an employee contracted for 3 days per week receives 12 days of PTO per year (20 × 3 ÷ 5).

Public holidays are given in addition to PTO and are not deducted from the PTO balance.

## Accrual

PTO accrues monthly. Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of PTO for each complete calendar month worked. Employees with 25 days of entitlement accrue 2.08 days per month.

Employees who join part way through a month begin accruing from the first day of the following month, plus a pro rata amount for the partial month, rounded to the nearest half day.

Accrual continues during paid leave, including PTO, paid sick leave and paid parental leave. Accrual is paused during unpaid leave of more than 10 consecutive working days.

### Taking PTO Before It Has Accrued

Employees may take PTO before it has accrued, up to a negative balance of 3 days, with manager approval. If employment ends while the balance is negative, the value of the negative days is deducted from the final pay.

## Requesting PTO

All PTO must be requested and approved in the PeopleHub portal before it is taken. Verbal or email approval alone is not sufficient.

### Notice Periods

- For absences of up to 3 consecutive working days, submit the request at least 2 working days in advance.
- For absences of more than 3 consecutive working days, submit the request at least 2 weeks in advance.
- For absences of more than 10 consecutive working days, submit the request at least 6 weeks in advance and discuss coverage with your manager first.

Managers may accept shorter notice in exceptional circumstances, such as a family emergency.

### Manager Response

Managers must approve or decline a PTO request within 3 working days of submission. If a request is declined, the manager must give a reason in PeopleHub and suggest alternative dates where possible.

Requests are generally approved on a first-come, first-served basis. Managers may decline requests when too many people in a team would be absent at the same time, or during published business-critical periods such as the year-end close for the Finance team.

### Minimum and Maximum Blocks

PTO may be taken in half-day or full-day units. No more than 15 consecutive working days of PTO may be taken in one block without approval from your department head.

## Carry-over

Employees are encouraged to use their full PTO entitlement each year.

Up to 5 unused days of PTO may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of that year. Any carried-over days not used by 31 March are forfeited.

Unused days above the 5-day carry-over cap are forfeited at the end of the calendar year, unless an exception applies.

### Exceptions to Carry-over

If you were unable to take PTO because of sickness or parental leave, you may carry over all unused days. These days must be used within 12 months of your return to work. Ask the People team to record the exception in PeopleHub.

## Cancelling or Changing Approved PTO

You may cancel approved PTO in PeopleHub at any time before it starts. The days are returned to your balance.

Acme Corp will only ask you to cancel approved PTO in a genuine business emergency. If this happens, Acme Corp will reimburse non-refundable travel or accommodation costs you can document, through the expense process in the Travel and Expense policy (POL-06).

## Falling Ill During PTO

If you become ill during PTO, tell your manager as soon as possible. The days of illness will be recorded as sick leave rather than PTO, and the PTO days will be returned to your balance, provided you meet the notification and evidence rules in the Sick and Family Leave policy (POL-04).

## PTO and Public Holidays

If a public holiday falls during a period of PTO, the public holiday is not deducted from your PTO balance.

## PTO During Probation and Notice

Employees on probation may take accrued PTO, subject to the normal approval process.

During a notice period, your manager may ask you to take some or all of your remaining PTO, or may agree that it will be paid out instead.

## Payment on Leaving

When employment ends, any accrued and unused PTO for the current year, plus any valid carried-over days, is paid out in the final pay at the employee's normal daily rate. Negative balances are deducted as described above.

## Purchasing Additional PTO

Once per year, during the benefits enrolment window described in the Benefits and Wellness policy (POL-12), full-time employees may buy up to 5 additional days of PTO. The cost is deducted from salary in equal amounts over the following 12 months. Purchased days cannot be carried over.

## Records and Balances

Your current balance, accrued days, carried-over days and pending requests are visible in PeopleHub. If you believe your balance is wrong, raise a PeopleHub ticket with the People team within 30 days of noticing the error.

## Managers' Responsibilities

Managers must:

- respond to every request within 3 working days;
- make sure each team member has taken at least 10 days of PTO by 30 September each year, and discuss plans with anyone who has not;
- avoid contacting employees on PTO except in a genuine emergency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I take PTO in hours?** No. The smallest unit is a half day.

**Can I donate PTO to a colleague?** No. PTO cannot be transferred between employees. Colleagues facing hardship can ask the People team about other forms of support, including the Employee Assistance Programme described in POL-12.

**Does PTO expire during the year?** No. Only carried-over days have a deadline (31 March).
