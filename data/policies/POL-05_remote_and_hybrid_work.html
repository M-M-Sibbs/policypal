Remote and Hybrid Work

This policy explains who can work remotely or on a hybrid pattern at Acme Corp, the rules that apply when working away from the office, and the support Acme Corp provides for home working. Policy owner: People team, with security requirements owned by the IT Security team.

Work Patterns

Acme Corp has three work patterns:

  • Office-based: the employee works from an Acme Corp office every working day.
  • Hybrid: the employee works from an office for a minimum of 2 days per week and remotely on the other days. This is the default pattern for most roles.
  • Fully remote: the employee works remotely on all working days and visits an office only for planned events.

Your work pattern is recorded in your employment contract or in a written variation agreed with your manager and the People team.

Eligibility

All employees start on the hybrid pattern unless their role is designated office-based. Employees become eligible to apply for a fully remote arrangement only after they have successfully completed their probation period (see the Employee Handbook Overview, POL-01) and have a performance rating of at least 3 ("Meets Expectations") in their most recent review (see POL-11).

Some roles are not eligible for fully remote work because they require physical presence, such as Facilities, hardware lab and reception roles.

Hybrid Anchor Days

Hybrid employees must work from the office on the team's anchor days. The company-wide anchor days are Tuesday and Thursday. Teams may choose a different pair of anchor days with department head approval, but every hybrid employee must still be in the office on at least 2 days per week.

Office attendance is averaged over a calendar month to allow for travel, leave and illness.

Core Hours

All hybrid and remote employees must be available for meetings and collaboration during core hours, which are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours employees may arrange their working time flexibly, within the 37.5-hour standard working week.

Employees working across time zones should agree an overlap of at least 3 hours per day with their immediate team.

Applying for Fully Remote Work

To apply for a fully remote arrangement, submit a "Remote work request" in PeopleHub. Your manager must respond within 10 working days. Approved arrangements start with a 3-month trial period, after which either side may request a review.

Acme Corp may end a fully remote arrangement with 30 days' written notice if business needs change or if the arrangement is not working well.

Home-Office Stipend

Acme Corp helps employees set up a safe and productive home workspace.

  • One-time setup stipend: hybrid and fully remote employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of $500 to buy a desk, chair, monitor or other equipment. It must be claimed within 90 days of starting a hybrid or remote arrangement, with receipts, through the expense process in the Travel and Expense policy (POL-06).
  • Monthly internet allowance: fully remote employees receive $40 per month towards home internet costs, paid automatically through payroll. Hybrid employees are not eligible for the monthly allowance.

Equipment bought with the setup stipend belongs to the employee. Company laptops and headsets remain company property.

Home Workstation Safety

Before starting hybrid or remote work, employees must complete the home workstation self-assessment in PeopleHub. It must be repeated every 12 months or after moving home. The Facilities team will contact you if the assessment shows a risk that needs attention.

Security When Working Remotely

Remote work must meet the same security standards as work in the office. In particular:

  • Always connect to company systems through the company VPN when using a network other than an Acme Corp office network.
  • Never use public computers, such as hotel business-centre machines, to access company systems.
  • Lock your screen whenever you step away, and do not let family members or visitors use your company laptop.
  • Avoid taking calls about Confidential or Restricted information in public places.

The full security rules, including password, multi-factor authentication and incident reporting requirements, are in the Information Security policy (POL-07).

Using Personal Devices When Working Remotely

Working remotely does not change the rules on personal devices. A personal laptop may not be used for company work, whether at home or elsewhere; employees must use their company-issued laptop. Personal phones and tablets may be used only for email and chat through the company's managed app, as set out in the Acceptable Use of IT policy (POL-08).

Working Temporarily from Another Country

Employees may work temporarily from another country for up to 20 working days in a rolling 12-month period, with written approval from their manager and the People team. Requests must be made at least 4 weeks before travel so that tax, immigration and security checks can be completed.

Some countries are excluded for security or legal reasons; the current list is published on the intranet. Working abroad for more than 20 working days requires a formal international assignment agreement.

Travel to the Office

Commuting costs between home and an employee's normal office are not reimbursed. Fully remote employees who are asked to attend an office for a planned event may claim travel and accommodation costs under the Travel and Expense policy (POL-06).
