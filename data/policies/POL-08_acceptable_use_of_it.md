# Acceptable Use of IT

This policy explains how employees may use Acme Corp's IT equipment, systems and networks, and when personal devices may be used for work. It complements the Information Security policy (POL-07), which sets the technical security requirements.

Policy owner: IT team.

## Scope

This policy covers company laptops, phones, accounts, software, networks, email, chat, cloud services, and any personal device used to access company information.

## Company Equipment

Company equipment is provided for business use. Reasonable, occasional personal use of a company laptop or company email is allowed, provided it:

- does not interfere with your work or the work of others;
- does not involve any prohibited activity listed in this policy;
- does not involve storing large personal files, such as personal photo or video libraries, on company devices.

Company equipment must not be used by family members, friends or anyone outside Acme Corp.

Do not take company equipment to a country on the excluded list published under the Remote and Hybrid Work policy (POL-05) without written approval from the IT Security team.

## Personal Devices (Bring Your Own Device)

### Personal Phones and Tablets

Employees may access company email and chat on a personal phone or tablet only through the company's managed work app, after enrolling the device in mobile device management (MDM). Enrolment gives IT the ability to wipe the work app and its data, but not your personal data.

Personal phones and tablets must have a screen lock with a PIN of at least 6 digits or biometric unlock, and must run an operating system version that still receives security updates.

### Personal Laptops and Computers

Personal laptops and computers must not be used for company work and must not be used to access company systems, other than the public careers site and the PeopleHub self-service portal through a web browser. This applies wherever you work, including from home.

### Company Data on Personal Devices

Never download, store or forward Confidential or Restricted information to a personal device, personal email account or personal cloud storage.

## Software

### Installing Software

Only software from the company software catalogue may be installed on company devices. To request software that is not in the catalogue, raise an IT Service Desk ticket. The IT team responds to software requests within 2 working days, and may require a security review before approval.

### Prohibited Software

The following software is prohibited on company devices and must not be used for company work:

- peer-to-peer file sharing and torrent clients;
- unapproved remote-access or remote-desktop tools;
- cryptocurrency mining software;
- tools designed to bypass security controls, such as VPN blockers, proxy anonymisers or password crackers (except for authorised security testing by the IT Security team);
- pirated or unlicensed software of any kind;
- browser extensions that are not in the approved extensions list.

### AI Tools

Only AI tools approved by the IT Security team may be used with company information. The approved list is published on the intranet. Confidential or Restricted information must never be entered into a public or unapproved AI tool.

## Email and Messaging

Use your company email and chat accounts for all work communication. Do not use personal messaging apps to discuss customer or Confidential information.

Company email must not be used to send chain letters, offensive content or mass personal messages. Auto-forwarding company email to an external address is blocked and must not be attempted.

## Internet Use

Do not access, download or share content that is illegal, sexually explicit, violent, discriminatory or otherwise offensive. Gambling websites must not be accessed from company devices or networks.

## Social Media

Do not post Confidential information about Acme Corp, its customers or colleagues on social media. If you mention your work at Acme Corp, make clear that your views are your own. Only the Communications team may speak on behalf of Acme Corp in public.

## Monitoring

To protect its systems and information, Acme Corp monitors the use of company devices, accounts and networks, including web traffic, email security scanning and device health. Monitoring is carried out in line with the law and with the Data Privacy and Retention policy (POL-09). Access to the content of individual communications requires approval from the Legal team and is only used for security investigations or legal obligations.

Employees should not expect privacy in any personal content they store on company devices.

## Returning Equipment

All company equipment must be returned within 5 working days of your last day of employment, as described in the Employee Handbook Overview (POL-01). Do not wipe a company device yourself; IT will do this securely.

## Reporting Misuse

Report suspected misuse of IT resources to the IT Service Desk or, if it may be a security incident, to the IT Security team as set out in POL-07.
