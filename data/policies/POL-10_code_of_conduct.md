# Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment

This policy sets out the standards of behaviour Acme Corp expects from everyone who works here, explains what harassment and discrimination are, and describes how to raise a concern and how concerns are investigated.

Policy owner: People team, with the Legal team.

## Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns and temporary workers, and to behaviour at work, at work events (including social events and business trips), and online in any space connected to work, such as company chat and social media where colleagues are involved.

## Our Standards of Behaviour

Everyone at Acme Corp must:

- treat colleagues, customers, suppliers and visitors with dignity and respect;
- act honestly and with integrity in all business dealings;
- follow the law and all Acme Corp policies;
- protect company and customer information (see POL-07 and POL-09);
- speak up when something seems wrong.

## Harassment, Bullying and Discrimination

Acme Corp does not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination of any kind.

**Discrimination** is treating someone less favourably because of a protected characteristic, such as age, disability, gender identity, marital or family status, pregnancy, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion or belief, sex, or sexual orientation.

**Harassment** is unwanted conduct related to a protected characteristic that violates someone's dignity or creates an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating or offensive environment. It can be a single serious incident or a pattern of behaviour.

**Sexual harassment** includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favours, sexual comments or jokes, displaying sexual images, and unwanted physical contact.

**Bullying** is offensive, intimidating, malicious or insulting behaviour, or an abuse of power, that undermines or humiliates someone.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include slurs and offensive jokes, mocking someone's accent or disability, repeated unwanted invitations, exclusion from meetings or information without reason, and spreading rumours.

## Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest arises when your personal interests could influence, or appear to influence, your decisions at work. Examples include hiring a relative, having a financial interest in a supplier, or working for a competitor in your spare time.

Declare any actual or potential conflict of interest to your manager and the Legal team through the conflict-of-interest form on the intranet within 10 working days of becoming aware of it.

## Gifts and Hospitality

You may accept gifts or hospitality from customers or suppliers only if they are modest, occasional and not intended to influence a business decision. The limit is $50 in value per gift. Any gift or hospitality above $50 must be declared to the Legal team and may only be accepted with their approval. Cash or cash equivalents, such as gift cards, must never be accepted.

Never offer or accept anything of value to improperly win or keep business. Bribery in any form is prohibited.

## Reporting Channels

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this policy, please report it. You can use any of these reporting channels:

- **Your manager**, unless your manager is involved in the concern.
- **The People team**, through a confidential PeopleHub case or in person.
- **The Ethics Hotline**, available 24 hours a day by phone on 0800-555-0199 or through the hotline website. The hotline is run by an independent provider and allows anonymous reports.
- **The Ethics mailbox**, ethics@acme.example, monitored by the Legal team.

Concerns about senior leaders may be raised directly with the Chair of the Audit Committee through the Ethics Hotline.

## Investigation Process

### Acknowledgement

Every report is acknowledged within 2 working days of receipt. Anonymous reports made through the Ethics Hotline are acknowledged through the hotline's secure message system.

### Assessment and Investigation

The People team, with the Legal team where needed, assesses each report and appoints an impartial investigator who has no involvement in the matter. The investigator interviews the person who raised the concern, the person the concern is about, and any witnesses, and reviews relevant documents.

Investigations aim to be concluded within 30 calendar days of the report. If an investigation will take longer, for example because of the number of witnesses, the people involved are told the reason and a revised date.

### Interim Measures

During an investigation, Acme Corp may take interim measures to protect those involved, such as changing reporting lines, adjusting work locations, or placing an employee on paid leave. Interim measures are not a finding of wrongdoing.

### Outcome

At the end of the investigation, the person who raised the concern and the person the concern was about are told whether the concern was upheld. Details of any disciplinary action are confidential to the person concerned.

Either party may appeal the outcome in writing to the People team within 10 working days of being told the outcome. Appeals are heard by a senior manager who was not involved in the original investigation.

## Confidentiality

Reports and investigations are handled as confidentially as possible. Information is shared only with those who need it to investigate or to take action. Investigation records are classified as Restricted and kept for 7 years after the investigation closes, as set out in POL-09.

## No Retaliation

Acme Corp strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or who takes part in an investigation. Retaliation includes dismissal, demotion, exclusion, threats, and negative changes to work assignments. Retaliation is itself a serious breach of this policy and will lead to disciplinary action.

Knowingly making a false report is also a breach of this policy.

## Disciplinary Action

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal. Gross misconduct includes harassment, discrimination, violence or threats of violence, theft, fraud (including false expense claims under POL-06), and serious breaches of information security.

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers must model the behaviour in this policy, take every concern seriously, pass reports to the People team within 1 working day, and never try to investigate a concern themselves.

## Training

All employees complete conduct and anti-harassment training within 30 days of joining and every 2 years after that. Managers complete an additional module on handling concerns.
