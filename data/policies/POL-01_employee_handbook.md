# Employee Handbook Overview

This handbook gives every Acme Corp employee a single starting point for how we work. It summarises the rules that apply to everyone and points to the detailed policies that govern specific topics such as leave, expenses, security and conduct. Where this overview and a detailed policy differ, the detailed policy applies.

Acme Corp is a fictional company. This document and every other policy in this collection are original, synthetic documents written for the PolicyPal project and may be freely used, copied and modified for that purpose.

## Purpose and Scope

This handbook applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees of Acme Corp, whether they work full time or part time, in an office or remotely. Contractors and agency workers are covered only where their contract says so.

The handbook is not a contract of employment. Acme Corp may update it at any time. Material changes are announced on the company intranet at least 14 calendar days before they take effect, unless a change is required sooner by law.

Each policy has an owner. The People team owns this handbook. Questions about a specific policy should go first to your manager and then to the policy owner named in that document.

## Our Values

Acme Corp expects everyone to work according to four values.

- **Customers first.** We make decisions that help our customers succeed, even when it is harder for us.
- **Own the outcome.** We take responsibility for our work from start to finish, and we raise problems early.
- **Be candid and kind.** We give direct, honest feedback and we deliver it with respect.
- **Learn every day.** We share what we know and we treat mistakes as a chance to improve.

These values are used in hiring, in performance reviews and in promotion decisions. The Performance Reviews and Promotion policy (POL-11) explains how.

## Working Hours

The standard full-time working week at Acme Corp is 37.5 hours, worked Monday to Friday. Standard office hours are 09:00 to 17:30, including an unpaid lunch break of 60 minutes.

Teams may agree flexible start and finish times with their manager, provided that the team covers its customer commitments and every employee is available during the company core hours. Core hours for hybrid and remote employees are defined in the Remote and Hybrid Work policy (POL-05).

Part-time employees agree their working pattern in writing with their manager. Their leave entitlements are calculated pro rata.

### Overtime

Salaried employees are not normally paid for overtime. If you regularly work more than 45 hours in a week for more than four consecutive weeks, your manager must review your workload with you and record the outcome.

Hourly-paid employees are paid for approved overtime at 1.5 times their normal hourly rate. Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager, except in an emergency affecting customers or safety.

### Timekeeping

Employees in roles that record time must submit their timesheet in the PeopleHub portal by 12:00 on the last working day of each week. Late timesheets may delay pay.

## Probation

All new employees serve a probation period of six months from their start date. During probation your manager will hold at least three formal check-ins: at the end of month one, month three and month five.

At the end of probation your manager will confirm in writing that you have passed, or will explain why probation is being extended or why employment will end. Probation may be extended once, by up to three months, where more time is needed to assess performance or where there has been significant absence.

During probation the notice period on either side is two weeks. After probation the notice period is one month, unless your employment contract states a longer period.

Employees on probation are not eligible for remote work arrangements beyond the standard hybrid pattern (see POL-05) and cannot apply for internal transfers.

## Employment Categories

- **Full-time employees** work the standard 37.5-hour week.
- **Part-time employees** work fewer than 37.5 hours per week on a fixed pattern.
- **Fixed-term employees** are employed until a specified end date or the completion of a specified project.
- **Interns** are employed on a fixed-term basis for a training programme of no more than 12 months.

## Code of Conduct Summary

Every employee must act honestly, treat colleagues and customers with respect, protect company information and avoid conflicts of interest. The full rules, including how to report concerns and how complaints are investigated, are set out in the Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment policy (POL-10).

Behaviour that may lead to disciplinary action includes dishonesty, harassment, discrimination, misuse of company systems, breaches of confidentiality, and working while impaired by alcohol or drugs.

## Dress Code

Acme Corp's dress code is smart casual. Employees meeting customers in person should dress in line with the customer's own standards. Clothing must be safe for the work being performed and must not carry offensive images or slogans. Reasonable adjustments are made for religious, cultural, medical and disability-related requirements.

## Equipment and Workspace

New employees receive a company laptop, a headset and access to the systems needed for their role on their first day. Equipment remains company property and must be returned within five working days of your last day of employment.

The rules for using company equipment and personal devices are in the Acceptable Use of IT policy (POL-08). Security requirements such as passwords and multi-factor authentication are in the Information Security policy (POL-07).

## Pay

Employees are paid monthly, on the 25th day of each month. When the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, pay is issued on the previous working day. Payslips are available in PeopleHub.

Errors in pay must be reported to the Payroll team within 30 days of the pay date so they can be corrected in the next pay run.

## Health and Safety

Everyone is responsible for working safely. Report hazards, accidents and near misses to your manager and to the Facilities team on the day they occur. Each office has trained first aiders and fire wardens, whose names are posted beside the main entrance.

Employees working from home must keep their workspace safe and complete the home workstation self-assessment described in POL-05.

## Personal Information Changes

Keep your address, bank details, emergency contact and legal name up to date in PeopleHub. Changes to bank details made after the 15th of a month may not take effect until the following month's pay run.

## Leaving Acme Corp

When you resign, give written notice to your manager and copy the People team. Your manager will arrange a handover plan. On your last day you must return all equipment, access cards and company documents, and your accounts will be disabled at 18:00.

An exit interview is offered to every departing employee. Attendance is voluntary and feedback is reported to leadership only in anonymised form.
