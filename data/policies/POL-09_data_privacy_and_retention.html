Data Privacy and Retention

This policy explains how Acme Corp classifies information, how personal data about customers, employees and others must be handled, and how long different records are kept before they are deleted. Policy owner: the Data Protection Officer (DPO), supported by the Legal team.

Privacy Principles

Acme Corp collects and uses personal data only when it has a lawful reason to do so. Everyone who handles personal data must follow these principles:

  • Collect only the personal data needed for a clear, stated purpose.
  • Use personal data only for the purpose it was collected for.
  • Keep personal data accurate and up to date.
  • Keep personal data only for as long as the retention schedule in this policy allows.
  • Protect personal data with appropriate security, as set out in the Information Security policy (POL-07).

Data Classification Levels

All Acme Corp information belongs to one of four classification levels. The owner of a document or dataset is responsible for classifying it. If you are unsure, treat the information as Confidential until the owner confirms its level.

LevelDescriptionExamples
PublicApproved for release outside Acme Corp.Published marketing content, the public careers site, press releases.
InternalFor Acme Corp employees and authorised contractors only. Limited harm if disclosed.Intranet pages, internal announcements, most policies.
ConfidentialRestricted to people with a business need. Disclosure could harm Acme Corp, customers or employees.Customer contracts, financial forecasts, source code, customer personal data.
RestrictedThe most sensitive information. Access is limited to named individuals.Employee health and medical records, payroll and bank details, security keys, information about ongoing investigations.

Handling Rules by Level

  • Public: no special handling.
  • Internal: keep within company systems; do not share externally without the owner's approval.
  • Confidential: store only in approved company systems; encrypt when sending outside Acme Corp; share externally only under a signed non-disclosure agreement or contract.
  • Restricted: all Confidential rules apply, plus access is limited to named individuals approved by the data owner, and access is logged and reviewed every 3 months.

Employee Personal Data

Acme Corp processes employee personal data to manage employment, pay, benefits, performance and legal compliance. Employee records are held in PeopleHub. Employees may view most of their own data in PeopleHub and must keep it up to date (see POL-01).

Requests from Individuals

Customers, employees and other individuals may ask to access, correct or delete their personal data. Forward any such request to privacy@acme.example on the day you receive it. Acme Corp must respond to a data subject request within 30 calendar days of receiving it. Do not respond to the request yourself.

Legal Holds

The Legal team may place a legal hold on records that are relevant to a legal claim, investigation or regulatory request. Records under a legal hold must not be deleted, even if their retention period has ended, until the Legal team lifts the hold.

Secure Deletion

Electronic records are deleted using the deletion tools approved by IT. Paper records are destroyed in the secure shredding bins. Do not keep personal copies of records beyond their retention period.

Privacy Incidents

Any incident that may affect personal data, such as sending a customer file to the wrong person, must be reported as a security incident under the Information Security policy (POL-07). The DPO assesses each personal data incident and, where the law requires, notifies the relevant regulator within 72 hours of Acme Corp becoming aware of it, and notifies affected individuals without undue delay.

Sharing Data with Third Parties

Personal data may be shared with suppliers only under a written data processing agreement approved by the Legal team. Transfers of personal data to another country require prior approval from the DPO.

Privacy by Design

Any new system, product feature or project that involves personal data must complete a privacy impact assessment with the DPO before development starts. High-risk processing, such as large-scale monitoring or processing of health data, requires formal sign-off by the DPO.

Training

All employees must complete data privacy training within 30 days of joining and every 12 months after that. This training is combined with the annual security awareness training described in POL-07.
