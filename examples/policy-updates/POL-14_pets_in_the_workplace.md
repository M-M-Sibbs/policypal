# Pets in the Workplace

This policy explains when employees may bring a dog to an Acme Corp office, how to register a pet, and the rules that keep the office safe and comfortable for everyone. It applies to all employees working from an Acme Corp office.

Policy owner: Facilities team.

## Which Pets Are Allowed

Only dogs are allowed in Acme Corp offices. Cats, birds, reptiles and other animals are not permitted.

Each employee may bring at most 1 dog at a time. Dogs must be at least 6 months old and fully house-trained.

Assistance dogs are not covered by this policy. They are welcome in every office at all times and do not need to be registered.

## Pet-Friendly Days

Dogs may come to the office on Wednesdays and Fridays only. These are the company's pet-friendly days.

Dogs are not allowed in the office during the year-end office closure or on days when customers visit the office for a scheduled event. The Facilities team publishes these dates on the intranet at least 5 working days in advance.

## Registering Your Dog

Before bringing a dog to the office for the first time, register it in PeopleHub using the request type "Office pet registration". Registration must be completed at least 10 working days before the first visit.

Registration requires:

- proof of up-to-date vaccinations, including rabies;
- proof of pet liability insurance with cover of at least $1,000,000;
- written approval from your manager;
- confirmation that no colleague in your immediate team has reported a dog allergy.

Registration is valid for 12 months and must then be renewed.

## Rules in the Office

Dogs must be kept on a lead no longer than 2 metres in all shared areas. Dogs are not allowed in kitchens, meeting rooms, bathrooms or the server room.

The owner is responsible for the dog at all times and must clean up after it immediately. Dogs may not be left alone in the office for more than 30 minutes.

Each floor has a designated pet area with water bowls. Dogs must use the outdoor relief area at the rear of the building.

## Allergies and Concerns

Employees with an allergy or fear of dogs can ask the Facilities team to designate their area as pet-free. The Facilities team will respond within 2 working days and will make the area pet-free while the request is reviewed.

Complaints about a specific dog are sent to the Facilities team through PeopleHub. After 2 upheld complaints within 6 months, the dog's registration is suspended for 3 months.

## Damage and Incidents

The owner is responsible for the cost of any damage caused by their dog. Any bite, injury or aggressive behaviour must be reported to the Facilities team on the same day. A dog involved in a bite incident may not return to the office.
