# Paid Time Off (PTO)

This is an updated version of the Paid Time Off policy, used to test PolicyPal's policy-update feature. It changes the carry-over limit and the deadline for using carried-over days. No version is given, so PolicyPal increases the current version automatically (1.0 becomes 1.1).

Policy owner: People team.

## Eligibility

All permanent and fixed-term employees are eligible for PTO from their first day of employment, including employees who are still on probation.

## Annual Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per calendar year. After five years of continuous service, the entitlement increases to 25 days per calendar year.

Part-time employees receive PTO pro rata to their contracted hours.

## Requesting PTO

All PTO must be requested and approved in the PeopleHub portal before it is taken. For absences of more than 3 consecutive working days, submit the request at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Carry-over

Up to 8 unused days of PTO may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 30 June of that year. Any carried-over days not used by 30 June are forfeited.

## Payment on Leaving

When employment ends, any accrued and unused PTO for the current year, plus any valid carried-over days, is paid out in the final pay at the employee's normal daily rate.
