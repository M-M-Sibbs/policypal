# Parking and Commuting

This policy explains who may park at Acme Corp offices, how parking permits are issued and what support is available for commuting. It is a sample policy for testing PolicyPal's policy-update feature.

## Parking Permits

Employees who work from an office at least 2 days per week may apply for a parking permit. Permits are requested in PeopleHub and are issued within 5 working days.

The headquarters car park has 120 spaces. Twelve spaces near the entrance are reserved for employees with a disability badge, and 8 spaces are reserved for electric-vehicle charging.

## Visitor Parking

Visitors must be registered at reception at least 1 working day in advance. Visitor parking is limited to 4 hours per visit.

## Electric-Vehicle Charging

Charging is free for employees. Each charging session is limited to 3 hours so that other employees can charge.

## Cycling to Work

Employees who cycle to work may claim a cycling allowance of $20 per month through payroll. Secure bicycle storage and showers are available at headquarters.
